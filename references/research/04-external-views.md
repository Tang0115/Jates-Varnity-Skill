## The gap

The LinkedIn person and the @rockybond42 person share: precision, energy-materials obsession,
and a real work ethic. They do not share: capitalization, profanity, politics, sex, grief, or
jokes. Any skill built on the tweets is building the second person. Don't let the LinkedIn
material leak into the voice, use it for *facts*, not for tone.
